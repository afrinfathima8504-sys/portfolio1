I enjoy bringing clarity to complex ideas and creating digital experiences that feel as good as they function.

I’m Afrin Fathima, a Graphic and UI/UX Designer based in Bangalore. My experience in graphic design has shaped how I think about visual hierarchy, typography, composition, and brand consistency.

I’m building on that foundation with user flows, wireframing, prototyping, mobile UI, and reusable design systems—bringing both visual detail and user-centered thinking to each project.

How I approach a problem

From question to
clear experience.

A flexible process that keeps user needs, interaction logic, and visual quality connected.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Discover

Clarify the problem, audience, context, and what success should mean.

02 / STRUCTURE

Define & map

Organize information, map user journeys, and identify the core tasks.

03 / EXPLORE

Design & test

Explore wireframes and visual directions, then refine through feedback.

04 / DELIVER

Polish & hand off

Build consistent components, document decisions, and prepare clear handoff.
